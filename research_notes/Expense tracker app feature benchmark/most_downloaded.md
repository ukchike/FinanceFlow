# Most downloaded income & expense tracker apps (Play Store / App Store)

Research date: 2026-11-26. IMPORTANT LIMITATION: play.google.com, appbrain.com and appgoblin.info could not be fetched from this session (blocked by network allowlist). All install figures below come from search-result snippets of third-party trackers, not live store pages, and should be re-verified against the live Google Play listings.

## What are the install counts and rating counts for each, with source and date?

### Takeaway
Best available evidence puts Realbyte's Money Manager first (roughly 10M–20M Android installs), followed by Monefy (~10M), Money Lover (~8M Android + ~3M iOS), Wallet by BudgetBakers (install count not confirmed), and Bishinews' Expense Manager (5M+). Trackers disagree, so treat the ranking as approximate.

### Cited Findings
- Money Manager Expense & Budget (Realbyte, com.realbyteapps.moneymanagerfree): AppGoblin ~20.8M installs; Grand Screen 20,096,232+; App Pricing Lab 10.0M; AppBrain 4.7 stars from ~449K ratings; last Play update reported Mar 9, 2026 — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.realbyteapps.moneymanagerfree); [Grand Screen](https://grand-screen.com/apps/money-manager-expense-budget/); [App Pricing Lab](https://apppricinglab.com/app/google_play/com.realbyteapps.moneymanagerfree)
- Realbyte also has a separate paid iOS listing "Money Manager (Remove Ads)" (id564730202) with its own review pool — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id564730202)
- Monefy (com.monefy.app.lite): Grand Screen 9,849,717+ Android installs (page dated 11.01.2025); a mirror of Monefy's site claims 11M+ downloads (low reliability); AppGoblin ~514K iOS installs — [Grand Screen](https://grand-screen.com/apps/monefy-budget-expenses-app/); [AppGoblin iOS](https://appgoblin.info/apps/1212024409)
- Money Lover (Finsify, com.bookmark.money): AppGoblin 8.3M Android installs, 3.3M iOS installs and ~627K iOS MAU (estimates); developer claims "over 10 million users" — [AppGoblin Android](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.bookmark.money); [AppGoblin iOS](https://appgoblin.info/apps/486312413); [AppBrain dev](https://www.appbrain.com/dev/Finsify/)
- Expense Manager (Bishinews): Play India rating 4.4, 5M+ downloads (per a 2026 review article) — [Expanager guide](https://www.expanager.in/guides/best-expense-tracker-apps-india)
- Wallet by BudgetBakers (com.droid4you.application.wallet): Similarweb tracks it and lists Monefy as a top competitor, but no install figure surfaced — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/app/google/com.droid4you.application.wallet/)
- Mint was discontinued by Intuit in 2024, so it is out of the ranking — [search summary of 2026 roundups](https://www.nerdwallet.com/finance/learn/best-expense-tracker-apps)
- Sensor Tower publishes quarterly "top 5 personal finance budgeting" download rankings by country (e.g. Q4 2025 Mexico Android) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-android-top-5-personal-finance-budgeting-and-planning-units-mx-63e363f0e1714cfff1979caa)

### Inferences
- Realbyte Money Manager is very likely #1 among pure trackers. Monefy and Money Lover probably sit around the 10M tier.
- The 5th slot is uncertain: Wallet (BudgetBakers), Bishinews Expense Manager, 1Money or Spendee are plausible.

### Gaps
- Live Google Play install tiers (10M+/50M+) were not verifiable; fetching play.google.com was blocked.
- App Store rating counts were not obtained for any app (apps.apple.com pages were not fetched).
- No install data was found for Wallet, Spendee, 1Money, Bluecoins, AndroMoney or Goodbudget. From prior knowledge Wallet is often listed at 10M+ on Play, but this is UNVERIFIED.

## What are the standout features of each of the top 5?

### Takeaway
Money Manager wins on a double-entry-style asset/account model and PC Wi-Fi editing. Wallet wins on bank sync. Money Lover offers shared wallets and paid bank linking. Monefy is the minimalist, one-tap entry app. Expense Manager is fully free.

### Cited Findings
- Money Manager (Realbyte): PC Manager lets you view and edit data over Wi-Fi, sort by date, category or account group, and see account-balance graphs on a PC; no bank linking; one-time premium unlock — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id560481810); [moonproduct blog, low weight](https://moonproduct.pages.dev/insights/money-manager-app-vs-finley)
- Realbyte's sync service is sold as a subscription (US$2.49/month or $19.99/year) on a related listing — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id564730202)
- Wallet (BudgetBakers): bank sync is Premium-only and read-only; marketing claims 15,000+ institutions while the App Store listing says 4,000 banks; it routes data through an aggregator — [Finder](https://www.finder.com/uk/budgeting/wallet-budgetbakers-review); [MakerStack](https://makerstack.co/reviews/wallet-by-budgetbakers-review/)
- Wallet pricing conflicts across sources: about €4.49/month ([Finny](https://getfinny.app/blog/wallet-budgetbakers-review-2026)); Premium $5.99 and Lifetime $29.99 ([MakerStack](https://makerstack.co/reviews/wallet-by-budgetbakers-review/)); older figures of $4.49/month and $26.99/year ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/reviews/wallet-finance-management))
- Wallet is described as "best for travel and many currencies" and blends automatic bank import with budgeting — [Finny free apps 2026](https://getfinny.app/blog/best-free-expense-tracker-apps-2026)
- Monefy: free with ads; a one-time Pro (~$2.99 iOS / $1.99 Android) adds a passcode, dark mode, multi-currency and Dropbox sync; reviewers call it intuitive and lightweight — [omellody review](https://omellody.pages.dev/budget-apps/monefy/); [Softpedia](https://mobile.softpedia.com/android/Monefy-Android-Review-2301.html)
- Money Lover: Premium adds unlimited wallets, budgets, sync, a web app, CSV export and removes ads — [Freedom251](https://freedom251.com/apps/money-lover/)
- Money Lover lifetime Premium costs €22.99 on the Spanish App Store — [finantres](https://finantres.com/?p=526999)
- Money Lover supports shared wallets with family and friends — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id486312413?l=en-us)
- Money Lover's bank sync ("Linked Wallet") is a separate paid service, not part of Premium (official help center) — [Money Lover Help](https://moneylover.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/35707018765209)
- Money Lover offers multi-device sync, multiple currencies and recurring-transaction alerts — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/money-lover-vs-spendee)
- Expense Manager (Bishinews): its listing says "100% FREE – full features, no hidden charges or in-app purchases"; the app is ad-supported — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.expensemanager&hl=en); [Expanager](https://www.expanager.in/guides/best-expense-tracker-apps-india)
- Spendee (runner-up): Basic, Plus and Premium tiers; the free tier gives 1 wallet, manual entry and charts, with no ads, and lacks bank sync and auto-categorization — [Millennial Money](https://millennialmoney.com/spendee-review); [Finny](https://getfinny.app/blog/best-free-expense-tracker-apps-2026)

### Inferences
- The pricing models split clearly. Money Manager, Monefy and Expense Manager use free-with-ads plus a one-time unlock or fully free. Wallet and Spendee use subscriptions, with bank sync as the paywall. Money Lover is a hybrid: lifetime Premium plus a separately paid bank-sync subscription.

### Gaps
- I found no sourced detail on AI features, SMS or notification parsing, receipt scanning, widgets or gamification for any of the five.
- I found no sourced security details (e.g. encryption) beyond Monefy's passcode and Wallet's read-only bank access.

## What do users praise and complain about most?

### Takeaway
Users praise simplicity and fast entry. The common complaints are sync reliability (bank sync for Wallet, device sync for Realbyte and Money Lover), a learning curve, and support quality.

### Cited Findings
- Realbyte: users complain they cannot sync between devices (PC, iPad, phone), that the UI "takes a bit to get used to", and that transactions can't be split — [App Store reviews via search](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id564730202); [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/560481810/haushaltsbuch-money-manager/reviews)
- Wallet: specific banks fail to sync (e.g. UBank in Australia), users want more frequent sync refresh, and support is slow — [Trustpilot](https://dk.trustpilot.com/review/budgetbakers.com); [Choice](https://choice.com.au/products/money/financial-planning-and-investing/creating-a-budget/budgetbakers-wallet-budget-money-manager)
- Monefy: praised for a clean interface and easy entry; users request savings goals and credit-card or loan tracking; an aggregator reports about two-thirds of ~6,500 reviews are negative (treat with caution) — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1212024409/monefy-money-tracker/reviews)
- Money Lover: sync is "still spotty at times", especially on shared wallets — [Money Lover help/listing snippet](https://moneylover.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/39118321383577)
- Spendee: buggy on Android, limited bank sync, a lifetime purchase that support didn't honor, and users asking for 2FA — [Millennial Money](https://millennialmoney.com/spendee-review); [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/spendee.com); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/spendee/reviews)

### Inferences
- Reliable sync, whether between devices or with banks, is the most common source of user complaints in this category.

### Gaps
- No systematic review-sentiment analysis (e.g. AppFollow or unitQ scores) was retrieved. The unitQ scorecard exists but wasn't fetched: [unitQ](https://unitq.com/unitq-scorecards/moneymanagerfree)

### Takeaway
The table stakes are manual entry, categories, budgets, charts, multi-currency, a passcode and backup. The few distinctive features found are listed below.

### Cited Findings
